# Dumpster Duke — game description

## Short (one line)

Crown the king of trash: tumbling pay-anywhere wins, Bottle Cap multipliers up to 100x and a Midnight Raid with a Loot Bag that never resets, for wins up to 10,000x.

## Full

Deep beneath the city, in an old mine stuffed with gold and glowing crystals, a cigar-chomping dumpster in a jewelled crown holds court. Meet the **Dumpster Duke**, and his crew of alley critters guarding the night's haul.

Dumpster Duke is a 6x5 pay-anywhere slot. Land 8 or more matching symbols anywhere to win. Winning symbols explode and new ones tumble in, again and again, for as long as wins keep coming. **Bottle Caps** worth 2x to 100x stick around through every tumble and multiply the spin's total win.

Land 4 or more **Golden Trash Cans** to start the **Midnight Raid**, worth 10, 12 or 15 free spins. Every Bottle Cap that lands on a winning free spin goes into the Duke's **Loot Bag**, and the Loot Bag never resets until the raid is over. Land 3 more Golden Trash Cans during the raid for +5 spins.

Feeling lucky? Switch on **Double Chance** to play at 1.25x the bet and trigger the Midnight Raid twice as often, or buy the raid outright for 100x the bet.

## Key features

- 6x5 grid, pay anywhere (8+ symbols), tumbling wins
- Bottle Cap multipliers from 2x to 100x
- Midnight Raid free spins with a persistent Loot Bag multiplier
- Double Chance (ante): 1.25x the bet, bonus twice as often
- Feature Spin: 5x the bet, every spin lands 3 Golden Trash Cans and a Bottle Cap
- Bonus buys: Midnight Raid (100x), Super Bonus with a x10 Loot Bag (250x), Mystery Bonus (100x)
- Max or Nothing: one 500x spin that pays the 10,000x max win or nothing
- Max win: 10,000x the bet
- RTP: 96.50% (all modes)
- Volatility: high
